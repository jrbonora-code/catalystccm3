Why Catalyst

A lender that understands the job site.

Catalyst Capital Management is a Tampa-based investment manager with more than 30 years of lending and finance experience, focused on construction and development. Our affiliate, Catalyst Community Development, builds multifamily and mixed-use projects across Florida, so we underwrite your budget and schedule the way a builder reads them.

We fill the gaps banks leave: projects that need speed, leverage or a structure that doesn’t fit a credit box. When we issue terms, we intend to close on them.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Who can borrow from Catalyst?

Builders, developers and real estate investors borrowing through a single-asset entity for a business purpose. We do not lend on owner-occupied homes.

Where do you lend?

Florida is our focus, and we lend across the Southeast United States.

Do I need prior building or flipping experience?

Experience is a central part of our underwriting, alongside the project itself, your equity and your team. Share your completed projects in the application and we will tell you early and plainly how your deal fits.

How are construction and rehab funds released?

Through draws administered by our own team. You submit a draw request, we verify the work completed and release funds against your approved budget. How draws work.
