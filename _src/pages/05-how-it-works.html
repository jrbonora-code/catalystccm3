Construction & rehab draws

How draws work

Construction and renovation funds are held by Catalyst and released as work is completed, measured against the budget approved at closing.

  1. Submit a draw request listing the budget line items completed, with invoices or photos as requested.
  2. Progress inspection. We verify completed work on site against the request and the approved budget.
  3. Review and approval. Our team reconciles the request with the inspection and any lien waivers required.
  4. Funds released to you or your contractor, and the loan balance updates to reflect the advance.

Draw frequency, inspection requirements and documentation are set out in your loan agreement.

Documents

What we typically request

  • Purchase contract or settlement statement
  • Line-item budget or scope of work
  • Plans, permits and schedule (construction)
  • General contractor information
  • Schedule of completed projects
  • Schedule of real estate owned
  • Borrowing entity documents
  • Recent bank statements
  • Personal financial statement
  • Evidence of insurance at closing

You don’t need all of this to apply. Start with the basics and we will request the rest as underwriting progresses.
