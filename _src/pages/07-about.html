About Catalyst

Opportunistic capital for real estate entrepreneurs.

Catalyst Capital Management is a Tampa-based investment management firm focused on real estate loans and private credit, with more than 30 years of lending and finance experience and a focus on construction and development.

Our approach

We finance the deals others pass on.

Catalyst fills gaps in the capital markets, financing projects that conventional lenders, banks and institutional markets underserve. We compete on speed and certainty of execution, and on a willingness to structure around a good project rather than force it into a template.

We operate across a platform of managed funds and strategic partnerships, which lets us fund and service loans directly. Our affiliate, Catalyst Community Development, develops multifamily, mixed-use and special-use projects in Florida, so our lending team works alongside people who build.

Catalyst Impact Capital Fund II, a $50 million fund, targets first mortgages in Florida, including ground-up construction, value-add projects and recapitalizations.
