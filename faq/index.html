General

What does Catalyst Capital Management finance?

On this platform, residential ground-up construction, fix-and-flip and bridge loans for investors and builders. Catalyst also offers commercial construction, SBA 504 and EB-5 financing; see catalystccm.com.

Is Catalyst a direct lender?

Yes. Loans are funded by investment funds managed by Catalyst and serviced in-house, so you work with the same team from application through payoff.

Where do you lend?

Florida is our focus, and we lend across the Southeast United States.

How is Catalyst different from a bank?

We focus on projects that need speed, leverage or a structure a bank won’t offer, and we underwrite the project and the sponsor together rather than running a deal through a fixed credit box.

Eligibility

Who can borrow?

Builders, developers and real estate investors borrowing through a single-asset entity, such as an LLC, for a business purpose.

Can I use a loan for a home I will live in?

No. All loans are business-purpose loans on non-owner-occupied property.

Do I need prior experience?

Experience matters. We weigh your track record together with the project, your equity, your liquidity and your contractor. List your completed projects in the application and we will give you a direct answer on fit early in the process.

Is there a minimum credit score?

Credit is one of several factors we review alongside experience, liquidity and the project itself. You can share an estimated credit range in the application; it is not a hard pull.

What property types are eligible?

Construction: single-family investment homes, townhomes and multifamily of five or more units. Fix & flip: 1–4 family residential. Bridge: single-family investment, multifamily of five or more units, and fully entitled land.

Rates & terms

What are your rates?

Construction loans float at SOFR plus 5.00%. Fix-and-flip loans are fixed, starting at 10%. Bridge loans are fixed or floating, starting at 9%. Your rate is set in your term sheet based on the project and sponsor.

How much can I borrow?

Construction up to 80% of cost and 70% of as-completed value; fix-and-flip up to 95% of cost and 75% of after-repair value; bridge up to 85% of cost and 75% of value. Try the loan estimator to see which limit applies to your deal.

What fees should I expect?

Construction and bridge loans carry an origination fee of 1% to 3%. Third-party costs such as appraisal, title, legal and inspections are typical. Your term sheet itemizes fees before you commit.

Is there a prepayment penalty?

Construction and bridge loans can be prepaid, subject to a minimum interest earned provision described in your term sheet.

Can I add preferred equity for more leverage?

On the right project, yes. Catalyst has paired senior construction loans with preferred equity to reach 95% of total cost.

Draws & servicing

Who services my loan?

Catalyst services its loans in-house, including draw administration.

How do draws work?

Submit a draw request for completed budget line items. We inspect the work, reconcile it with your approved budget and release funds. See the draw process.

What if my budget changes mid-project?

Talk to us early. Budget reallocations and change orders are reviewed against the remaining budget and the as-completed value.

Applying

How do I apply?

Complete the online application. It takes about ten minutes and asks for the property, your numbers, your exit and your contact information.

How long until I hear back?

A Catalyst lender reviews every application personally and will reach out to discuss next steps and any documents needed for a term sheet.

How long does closing take?

Bridge loans typically fund 30 to 45 days from application, and construction loans 30 to 60 days. Ready plans, budgets, title and entity documents shorten the timeline.

Do you work with brokers?

Brokers can submit through the online application by indicating that they are applying on behalf of a borrower.

Ready to put your next project in motion?

The online application takes about ten minutes. A Catalyst lender reviews every request personally and follows up with next steps.
