How borrowers use bridge

Common bridge scenarios

Land & lot acquisition

Close on fully entitled land or finished lots now, then transition into a construction loan when plans and permits are ready.

Time-sensitive purchases

Compete with cash buyers on auctions, off-market deals and short closing windows.

Refinance a maturing loan

Pay off a lender that is ready to exit and buy the time to finish, sell or stabilize.

Renovate & stabilize

Carry a rental property or small portfolio through renovation and lease-up before permanent financing.

Bridge FAQ

Questions about bridge loans

Can I use a bridge loan to buy land before I build?

Yes, for fully entitled land. Many builders use a bridge loan to secure the site, then move into a Catalyst construction loan once plans and permits are complete.

Fixed or floating?

Bridge loans are available on a fixed or floating basis, with rates starting at 9%. Your term sheet will specify the structure.

Do you lend in second position?

Bridge loans are typically first mortgages. We will also consider second mortgages and B notes on the right project.

How fast can you close?

Bridge loans typically fund 30 to 45 days from application. A clean title and a ready appraisal shorten that timeline.

Ready to put your next project in motion?

The online application takes about ten minutes. A Catalyst lender reviews every request personally and follows up with next steps.
