Bridge FAQ

Questions about bridge loans

Can I use a bridge loan to buy land before I build?

Yes, for fully entitled land. Many builders use a bridge loan to secure the site, then move into a Catalyst construction loan once plans and permits are complete.

Fixed or floating?

Bridge loans are available on a fixed or floating basis, with rates starting at 9%. Your term sheet will specify the structure.

Do you lend in second position?

Bridge loans are typically first mortgages. We will also consider second mortgages and B notes on the right project.

How fast can you close?

Bridge loans typically fund 30 to 45 days from application. A clean title and a ready appraisal shorten that timeline.
