Fix & flip loans

Acquire, renovate and sell with less cash in the deal.

High-leverage, fixed-rate financing for investors buying and renovating 1–4 family homes. Finance the purchase and the rehab together, then sell the property or rent it.

  • Up to 95% of total cost and 75% of after-repair value
  • Fixed rates from 10% with terms up to 24 months
  • Loan amounts from $350,000 to $5 million, first-mortgage secured

How leverage is measured

Your loan is the lesser of 95% of purchase price plus rehab budget, or 75% of the appraised after-repair value. Use the estimator below to see which limit applies to your deal.

Program highlights

Why investors flip with Catalyst

Up to 95% of cost

Keep more capital free for your next acquisition by financing nearly the full purchase and renovation.

Fixed-rate certainty

A fixed rate means your carry cost doesn’t move while the house is under renovation or on the market.

Rehab funding

Renovation dollars are released against your approved scope as the work is completed and verified.

Sell or rent

Exit through a sale, or stabilize the property as a rental and refinance into long-term financing.

Eligible projects

From cosmetic refresh to full gut rehab

  • Single-family homes, including condominiums and townhomes
  • Two- to four-unit properties held for sale or rent
  • Light and heavy rehab: kitchens, baths, systems, roofs and additions
  • Distressed and value-add acquisitions where speed wins the deal
  • Rehab-to-rent projects planning a long-term refinance

All properties must be non-owner-occupied and held in a borrowing entity.

Fix & flip FAQ

Questions investors ask

Does the loan cover renovation costs?

Yes. The loan can cover the purchase and the rehab budget together, up to 95% of total cost and 75% of the after-repair value.

What is the smallest loan you make?

Fix-and-flip loans start at $350,000. If your project is smaller, contact us and we will tell you whether another option fits.

Can I rent the property instead of selling it?

Yes. Many borrowers renovate, lease up and refinance into long-term rental financing before the loan matures.

What do you need to evaluate my deal?

The purchase contract, a scope of work and rehab budget, your estimate of after-repair value, and a summary of your recent projects. You can upload a budget or scope directly in the application.

Ready to put your next project in motion?

The online application takes about ten minutes. A Catalyst lender reviews every request personally and follows up with next steps.
