Ground-up construction loans

New construction financing, lot to certificate of occupancy.

First-mortgage construction loans for residential builders and developers: spec homes, infill and scrape-and-rebuild projects, townhomes and small multifamily. One loan covers land, hard costs and soft costs, with draws administered in-house.

  • Up to 80% of total cost and 70% of as-completed value
  • Interest-only for up to 36 months, paid on funds drawn
  • Preferred equity available to extend total leverage on qualified projects

Program highlights

Designed around how homes actually get built

Land through vertical

Finance the lot or land, hard costs and soft costs together, so one lender carries the project from closing to C.O.

Pay on what you draw

Interest-only payments accrue on funds disbursed, not on the full commitment, which keeps carry low early in the build.

In-house draws

Our team reviews draw requests and releases funds against your approved budget, with no outside servicer in the middle.

Builder-minded underwriting

We read budgets, schedules and plans the way a developer does, because our affiliate builds in Florida too.

What to have ready

Your construction loan package

You can apply with just the basics. During underwriting we typically ask for the following.

  • Plans and permits, or the status of permitting
  • Line-item budget covering hard and soft costs
  • Construction schedule and general contractor information
  • Purchase contract or settlement statement for the land
  • Schedule of completed projects and current real estate owned
  • Entity documents for the borrowing entity
  • Liquidity verification, such as recent bank statements
Construction FAQ

Questions builders ask

Can the loan include the cost of the land?

Yes. Land or lot cost, hard costs and soft costs can be financed together, subject to the 80% loan-to-cost and 70% as-completed value limits.

How is the rate set?

Construction loans float at SOFR plus 5.00%. Interest is charged on funds drawn, with interest-only payments over the term.

Can I pay the loan off early when homes sell?

Yes. Prepayment is permitted, subject to a minimum interest earned provision described in your term sheet.

Do you finance townhomes and multifamily?

Yes. Eligible projects include single-family investment homes, attached product and multifamily of five or more units.

How long does it take to close?

Construction loans typically fund 30 to 60 days from application. Having plans, a budget and permits in hand shortens that timeline.

Ready to put your next project in motion?

The online application takes about ten minutes. A Catalyst lender reviews every request personally and follows up with next steps.
